Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 15, 2026

Welcome to SleekConnect ("we", "us", "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect and process the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address and password (encrypted) when you register.
  • Age Verification: Date of birth to verify that you are at least 18 years old.
  • Identity Verification: A live webcam selfie captured during registration for human identity verification.
  • Usage Data: Connection logs, timestamps, and session duration for platform analytics.
  • Cookies: Essential cookies for session management and, with your consent, analytics and advertising cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account
  • To verify your identity and prevent fake or underage accounts
  • To provide secure peer-to-peer video matching services
  • To enforce our Terms of Service and community guidelines
  • To communicate with you about your account status
  • To improve our platform and user experience
  • To display relevant advertisements (with consent, via Google AdSense)

3. Identity Verification Selfie

The webcam selfie captured during registration is used solely for identity verification purposes. Our process:

  • The selfie is securely stored during the agent review period
  • Upon approval or rejection, the image is permanently deleted from our servers
  • We do not use facial recognition technology or share selfies with any third party
  • Gallery image uploads are disabled — only live camera captures are accepted

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: For authentication, database, and secure file storage (hosted in compliance with SOC 2 Type II)
  • Google AdSense: For advertisements (subject to Google's Privacy Policy)
  • Google STUN Servers: For WebRTC peer connection establishment (no data is routed through our servers)

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies for:

  • Essential Cookies: Session management and authentication (always active)
  • Analytics Cookies: Usage patterns to improve the platform (with consent)
  • Advertising Cookies: Google AdSense for personalized ads (with consent)

You can manage cookie preferences at any time. Declining non-essential cookies will not affect core functionality.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • All connections are encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt
  • Video calls are peer-to-peer (WebRTC) — we do not record or intercept video streams
  • Agent access is restricted and monitored
  • Row Level Security (RLS) is enforced on all database tables

7. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • Withdraw consent for cookie tracking
  • Request a copy of your personal data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

8. Age Requirement

SleekConnect is strictly for users aged 18 and older. By registering, you confirm that you meet this age requirement. We verify age through date of birth submission during registration.

9. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained as long as your account is active
  • Verification selfies are deleted upon agent action (approval/rejection)
  • Session logs are retained for 30 days, then automatically purged
  • Upon account deletion, all data is permanently removed within 14 days

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of significant changes via email or platform notification. Continued use of SleekConnect after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact Us

For privacy-related inquiries, data requests, or concerns:

  • Email: sleekconnecto@gmail.com
